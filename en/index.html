Our process

From your electric bill to a clear proposal.

We ask for the technical information needed to start with a useful recommendation.

Share your usage

CFE bill and recent consumption history, property type, location and project objective.

We evaluate

We review usable roof area, shading and obstacles, orientation, roof/anchoring type, electrical phases, load center, grounding, cable route and inverter location.

Get a proposal

We present the proposed system, structure, protections, conduit, commercial model and project scope before approval.

Installation

We coordinate structure, module mounting, wiring, protections, testing and commissioning of the approved system.
